## About

DevOps Engineer at Swiftsoft Infotech with hands-on expertise in designing, automating, and operating scalable cloud infrastructure across AWS environments. I architect and maintain CI/CD pipelines that enable engineering teams to ship faster and with greater confidence, applying infrastructure-as-code principles to eliminate operational drift and reduce time-to-production. My engineering philosophy is rooted in observability, reliability, and automation — building systems that are predictable under load, transparent in failure, and efficient at scale.

I specialize in container orchestration with Kubernetes, configuration management with Ansible, and cloud-native deployment patterns on AWS. I treat every manual process as a bug and every repeated task as an automation opportunity. Currently deepening expertise in Terraform, Site Reliability Engineering, and distributed systems design.

**Open To:** Senior DevOps Engineer roles · Platform Engineering · Cloud Infrastructure · SRE positions · Remote & Hybrid engagements
